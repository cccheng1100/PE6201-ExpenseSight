# Corporate Travel & Expense Policy

**Example Company · Domestic Travel Policy · Effective 1 January 2026**

*Company-agnostic template; thresholds configurable per deployment.*

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy governs domestic business travel within mainland China. It applies to all full-time employees. Cross-border travel (including Hong Kong, Macau, Taiwan, and overseas) is governed by a separate international policy.

Local intracity transport within the employee's home city is **not** business travel and is not covered by this policy.

## 2. Pre-Approval (Mandatory Before Booking)

- A **Travel Application Form (出差审批单)** must be submitted and approved **before** any booking or travel commences.
- Backdated approvals are not accepted. A claim without an approved pre-approval ID, or whose travel dates fall outside the approval window, is flagged to Finance.
- The pre-approval must state: applicant, destination city(s), departure and return dates, intended transport mode, estimated total cost.
- Multi-city trips must list every leg in the pre-approval.
- Approvals are valid for the trip dates stated; extending the trip requires a new approval.

## 3. Accommodation Standard

Maximum nightly rate, inclusive of taxes and service charge:

| Grade | Beijing / Shanghai / Guangzhou / Shenzhen | All other cities |
|---|---|---|
| G1–G2 (Staff / Senior Engineer) | ¥450 / night | ¥380 / night |
| G3–G4 (Manager and above) | ¥550 / night | ¥450 / night |

- Stays beyond 14 consecutive nights at the same location require a second approval.
- Room upgrades, mini-bar, and laundry above ¥50 per stay are not reimbursable.
- Actual cost above the cap is borne by the employee (核减).
- **Every hotel stay requires both a VAT special invoice and a hotel folio (住宿清单).**

## 4. Daily Allowance (补助)

Per 24-hour period away from home city:

| City tier | Daily allowance |
|---|---|
| Beijing / Shanghai / Guangzhou / Shenzhen | ¥100 / day |
| All other cities | ¥80 / day |

- A half-day of travel pays 50%.
- The allowance is paid without receipts; actual meals are not separately reimbursed.

## 5. Transport

- **Cabin class is determined by grade, not by flight duration.**
- All grades fly **economy class (经济舱)** and take **high-speed rail second class (二等座)** by default.
- Business class (商务舱 / 商务座) or first class (一等座) above the employee's grade standard is **flagged for reviewer approval and cost analysis**, not auto-rejected. The employee files it normally in the transport module; the system identifies the cabin from the ticket and records it for later analysis.
- **Prefer high-speed rail over flights** for city pairs within ~5 hours rail distance. Flight is allowed when the airfare is lower than the equivalent HSR fare.
- Flight e-ticket itinerary (行程单) and railway receipts must be attached.

## 6. Multi-Leg Itinerary Consistency

For trips involving multiple flights, rail segments, or hotel stops:

- The **arrival city of leg N-1 must equal the departure city of leg N**.
- A gap longer than **12 hours** between legs must be explained as a required overnight stay.
- A leg ending in City A but the next departing from City B, with no connecting ground-transport receipt, is an **itinerary inconsistency**.
- Both original and rebooked tickets must be attached if a segment is changed.
- The claim's leg list must match the pre-approval's leg list; any unapproved additional leg is flagged.

## 7. Required Attachments

Every travel claim must include:

1. Approved Travel Application Form ID (出差审批单).
2. Transport receipts: flight itinerary (行程单), railway receipts, or road transport invoices.
3. For **every** hotel stay: hotel folio (住宿清单) and VAT special invoice.
4. For any other expense above ¥300: a valid VAT fapiao made out to the company.

## 8. Filing Deadline

- Claims should be filed within **60 calendar days** of the expense date.
- Claims beyond 60 days are flagged to Finance as a reminder, not auto-rejected.
- All expenses incurred in a calendar year must be claimed before year-end close.

## 9. Non-Reimbursable Items

- Travel claims must not include alcohol, entertainment, or personal expenses.
- Traffic fines and parking violation penalties are not reimbursable.
- Costs above the policy cap not covered by a filled-in 核减 deduction are not reimbursable.

## 10. Duplicate Claims

- The same expense may not be submitted twice.
- A claim whose employee and travel-date window overlaps with a previously submitted claim by the same employee is flagged as a potential duplicate.
- Per diem may not be claimed on dates that overlap with another approved trip.

## 11. Review and Appeal

- Claims returned by the pre-screening tool go back to the employee with a stated reason and the exact policy clause cited.
- The tool does not issue a final rejection; Finance makes the final decision.
